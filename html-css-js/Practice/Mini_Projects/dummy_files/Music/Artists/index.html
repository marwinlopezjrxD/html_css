About Lee Hi

Lee Ha-yi (born September 23, 1996), known professionally as Lee Hi, is a South Korean singer and songwriter. She rose to fame as the runner-up of K-pop Star Season 1 in 2011 and debuted under YG Entertainment in 2012 with the hit single "1, 2, 3, 4".

Known for her powerful, soulful voice and emotional delivery, Lee Hi has released acclaimed albums blending R&B, soul, and pop. After leaving YG in 2019, she signed with AOMG and released her third studio album 4 ONLY in 2021 — a deeply personal exploration of love's many faces.

In 2024, she joined duover and continues to captivate fans with her timeless vocals and heartfelt music.
