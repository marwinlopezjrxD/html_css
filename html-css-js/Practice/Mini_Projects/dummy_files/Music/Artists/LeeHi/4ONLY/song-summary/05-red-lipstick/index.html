Red Lipstick

Lee Hi

Track 05 • 4 ONLY (2021, AOMG)

Song Summary

"Red Lipstick" is the fierce, confident lead single from 4 ONLY, a powerful collaboration with rap legend Yoonmirae (Tasha). It's an anthem of self-love and bold femininity—Lee Hi embraces her allure, declaring she's "dangerous" and irresistible with that signature red lipstick as armor and weapon.

The lyrics celebrate owning your sensuality ("I'm a bad girl, red lipstick / I know you like it") while rejecting insecurity. Yoonmirae's commanding verse adds swagger and strength, turning the track into a glamorous, empowering strut. The production is sleek and addictive, blending trap beats with luxurious vibes.

Key vibe: Unapologetic glamour and queen energy—perfectly capturing the album's theme of embracing one's full, fearless self.
