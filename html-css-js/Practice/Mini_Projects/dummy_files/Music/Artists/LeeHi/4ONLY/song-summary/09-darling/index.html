Darling

Lee Hi

Track 09 • 4 ONLY (2021, AOMG)

Song Summary

"Darling" is a sweet, breezy mid-tempo love song where Lee Hi expresses pure affection and longing for her beloved. With light acoustic guitar and a relaxed groove, her warm vocals shine as she imagines spending lazy, intimate moments together—calling out "Darling" with playful tenderness.

The lyrics are simple yet heartfelt: wanting to stay close, share quiet days, and keep the connection alive even when apart ("Even if you're far away, call me darling"). It's a gentle celebration of being in love, free from the complications explored in earlier tracks.

Key vibe: Soft and romantic—like a sunny afternoon with someone special, full of cozy warmth and effortless charm.
