Song Summary

"What Is Love?" (어려워 – "It's Hard") is a tender, introspective R&B track where Lee Hi grapples with the overwhelming complexity of love. Produced once again by CODE KUNST, it features warm, jazzy chords and her signature emotive delivery as she confesses how love feels both beautiful and painfully confusing.

The lyrics capture the dizzying cycle of affection: "The more I like you, the harder it gets / What is this thing called love?" She questions why something so sweet can be so difficult, blending joy with anxiety in a deeply relatable way.

Key vibe: Soft and contemplative—Lee Hi's velvet vocals float over gentle instrumentation, creating an intimate late-night reflection on love's bittersweet nature.
