Intentions

Lee Hi

Track 02 • 4 ONLY (2021, AOMG)

Song Summary

"Intentions" is a sultry, Latin-inspired R&B track that captures the frustration and exhaustion of deciphering a partner's mixed signals in a complicated relationship. Lee Hi's husky vocals glide over bluesy acoustic elements, painting the lover's motives as an unreadable "labyrinth of thoughts" and "map that cannot be understood."

The lyrics cleverly critique manipulative dynamics—questioning why someone dances around feelings, toys with emotions, or tries to control appearances ("Why do men try to teach young women?"). It's a confident declaration of boundaries amid confusion, blending sensuality with sharp introspection.

Key vibe: Playful yet weary—perfectly showcasing Lee Hi's mature, velvety tone on the album's exploration of love's uncertainties.
