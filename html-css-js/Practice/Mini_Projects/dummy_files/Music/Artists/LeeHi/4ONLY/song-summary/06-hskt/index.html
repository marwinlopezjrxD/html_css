Song Summary

"H.S.K.T." (머리어깨무릎발 / Head, Shoulders, Knees, Toes) is a playful, confident R&B duet where Lee Hi demands full-body, unapologetic love from head to toe. Drawing from the classic children's song, she flips it into a bold declaration: "Love me that much—from my head, shoulders, knees, to toes."

With sassy lyrics like "Sorry, not sorry," she embraces her allure without hesitation, urging her partner to be direct and obsessive. Wonstein's smooth rap verse adds flirtatious energy, turning the track into a fun, empowering exchange about wanting transparent, all-in affection.

Key vibe: Cheeky and addictive—pure self-love swagger with catchy hooks and groovy production that makes you move.
