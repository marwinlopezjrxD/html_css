Savior (feat. B.I)

Lee Hi

Track 01 • 4 ONLY (2021, AOMG)

Song Summary

"Savior" opens 4 ONLY with raw vulnerability, where Lee Hi pleads for a lover to be her guiding light amid heartbreak's shadows. Blending silky R&B hooks with B.I's introspective rap, the track explores redemption in flawed relationships—fearing loss yet craving salvation through connection. It's a soul-stirring anthem of emotional surrender, produced with intimate layers that echo the album's theme of love's quiet fears.

Key vibe: Healing through harmony, with gospel-infused choruses that hit like a warm embrace.
