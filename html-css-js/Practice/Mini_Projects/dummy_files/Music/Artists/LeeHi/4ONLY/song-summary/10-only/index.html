Song Summary

"ONLY" serves as the poignant closing track of 4 ONLY, a stripped-down ballad that reflects on a love so deep it becomes the sole focus of one's world. Written and composed by Ahn Shin-ae with philtre's delicate arrangement, Lee Hi's voice carries raw emotion over minimal piano and subtle strings.

The lyrics express total devotion and quiet longing: wanting to be someone's "only" everything—only love, only thought, only reason. It's a tender surrender to vulnerability, accepting that this person has become irreplaceable, even if the feeling isn't fully returned.

Key vibe: Intimate and bittersweet—a gentle fade-out that leaves you wrapped in the album's lingering warmth and emotional honesty.
