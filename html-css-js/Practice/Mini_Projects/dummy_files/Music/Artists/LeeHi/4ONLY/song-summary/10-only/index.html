ONLY

Lee Hi

Track 10 • 4 ONLY (2021, AOMG)

Song Summary

"ONLY" is the quiet, breathtaking closer to Lee Hi's 4 ONLY album—a stripped-down ballad that feels like a whispered confession in the dark. Written and composed by Ahn Shin-ae with philtre's delicate arrangement, the song builds on minimal piano and subtle strings, letting Lee Hi's raw, soaring vocals carry every ounce of emotion. It's a moment of complete surrender, where love becomes so consuming that one person turns into your entire world.

The lyrics paint a picture of total devotion: wanting to be someone's "only" everything—their only thought, only love, only reason to smile, even when you're far apart. There's a gentle ache in lines about accepting this depth of feeling, even if it's vulnerable or unreturned. Lee Hi delivers each note with heartbreaking sincerity, making the song feel deeply personal, as if she's singing directly to the listener.

As the final track, "ONLY" leaves a lingering warmth—a bittersweet fade-out that perfectly wraps the album's journey through love's fears, joys, and uncertainties. It's intimate, honest, and beautifully understated, reminding us how rare and precious it is to find someone who becomes your one and only.
