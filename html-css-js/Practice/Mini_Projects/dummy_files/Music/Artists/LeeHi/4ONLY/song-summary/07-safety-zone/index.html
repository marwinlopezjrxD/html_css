Safety Zone

Lee Hi

Track 07 • 4 ONLY (2021, AOMG)

Song Summary

"Safety Zone" is a dreamy, mid-tempo R&B ballad where Lee Hi yearns for a secure emotional space in a fragile relationship. Produced by CODE KUNST, the track features lush, atmospheric layers and her signature breathy vocals as she navigates the fear of getting too close.

Lyrics express the push-pull of love: wanting to dive deeper yet needing boundaries to feel safe ("Draw a safety zone around me / So I can breathe"). It's an intimate confession of vulnerability—hoping the partner will protect her heart while she cautiously opens up.

Key vibe: Warm yet cautious—gentle synths and soft beats create a comforting cocoon that mirrors the song's longing for emotional shelter.
