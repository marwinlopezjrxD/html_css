I Love Me After You

Mitski

Track 11 • The Land Is Inhospitable and So Are We (2023, Dead Oceans)

Song Summary

"I Love Me After You" is a triumphant, sensual closer — post-breakup rebirth. Mitski strides naked through the house, owning every corner, dancing in the dark, claiming the night as hers. The king is gone, but she remains, finally free to love herself completely.

With bold, swaggering energy and playful lyrics, the song flips the album's melancholy into empowerment. It's a celebration of solitude, self-possession, and finding joy in one's own company after loss.

Key vibe: Liberation, self-love as the ultimate victory, dancing alone in the dark.
