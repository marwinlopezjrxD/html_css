I Don't Like My Mind

Mitski

Track 04 • The Land Is Inhospitable and So Are We (2023, Dead Oceans)

Song Summary

"I Don't Like My Mind" is a raw confession of self-loathing and mental exhaustion. Mitski lists small regrets — eating the last slice of cake, taking a Christmas job for money — that her mind relentlessly replays, punishing her long after the moment has passed.

The song captures the cruelty of an overactive brain that won't let go of past mistakes, turning minor choices into endless torment. She craves escape ("Please don't take from me"), even offering to work through holidays just to quiet the noise inside.

Key vibe: Internal battle with rumination, the mind as an unforgiving enemy.
