My Love Mine All Mine

Mitski

Track 07 • The Land Is Inhospitable and So Are We (2023, Dead Oceans)

Song Summary

"My Love Mine All Mine" is a gentle, luminous declaration — the only thing Mitski truly owns in this world is her love. Nothing is forever, she sings: the moon will leave, money will run out, but her love belongs to her alone, and she sends it to the moon to shine on her beloved even after she's gone.

It's one of Mitski's most direct and tender songs, a quiet anthem of devotion that became a massive viral hit. The simplicity of the lyrics and the warm, swaying melody make it feel timeless — love as the one eternal possession in an impermanent life.

Key vibe: Profound, selfless love — the comfort of knowing something beautiful will outlast you.
