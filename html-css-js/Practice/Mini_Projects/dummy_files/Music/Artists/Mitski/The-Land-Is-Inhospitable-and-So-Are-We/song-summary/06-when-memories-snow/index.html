When Memories Snow

Mitski

Track 06 • The Land Is Inhospitable and So Are We (2023, Dead Oceans)

Song Summary

"When Memories Snow" is a swirling, overwhelming rush of recollection. Mitski compares memories to falling snow — soft at first, then piling up, burying everything beneath their weight. No matter how much you shovel them away, they keep coming back, heavier each time.

The song builds with layered vocals and instrumentation, mimicking the suffocating feeling of being trapped under the past. It's about the exhausting cycle of trying to move forward while old thoughts and regrets keep drifting down, covering the present.

Key vibe: The relentless accumulation of memory, the futility of trying to escape what’s already happened.
