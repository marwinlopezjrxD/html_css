Heaven

Mitski

Track 03 • The Land Is Inhospitable and So Are We (2023, Dead Oceans)

Song Summary

"Heaven" is a tender, swooning love song with a warm country twang — strings, pedal steel, and all. Mitski paints intimate moments in a roadside motel: tangled sheets, shared cigarettes, the glow of love making even a transient place feel eternal.

It's rare for Mitski to write something this openly romantic — finding paradise in the arms of a lover, where time slows and the outside world fades. The lyrics are full of small, sensual details: skin on skin, breath on neck, the quiet bliss of being fully seen and wanted.

Key vibe: Fleeting, fragile joy — love as a temporary shelter in an inhospitable world.
