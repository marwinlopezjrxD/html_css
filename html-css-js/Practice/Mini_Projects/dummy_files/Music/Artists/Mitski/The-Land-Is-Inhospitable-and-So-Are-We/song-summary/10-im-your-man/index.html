I'm Your Man

Mitski

Track 10 • The Land Is Inhospitable and So Are We (2023, Dead Oceans)

Song Summary

"I'm Your Man" is a dark, subversive confession — Mitski flips gender roles, admitting she's the destructive force in the relationship. She'll be whatever her lover needs, even if it means becoming the monster that ruins everything. The song builds with ominous strings and ends with dogs barking, symbolizing the inner demons she's unleashed.

It's a chilling acknowledgment of toxic patterns, self-awareness wrapped in devotion. Mitski offers herself fully, flaws and all, knowing she'll likely cause pain — yet still pleading to stay.

Key vibe: Twisted devotion, owning one's darkness in love, the terror of being the one who hurts.
