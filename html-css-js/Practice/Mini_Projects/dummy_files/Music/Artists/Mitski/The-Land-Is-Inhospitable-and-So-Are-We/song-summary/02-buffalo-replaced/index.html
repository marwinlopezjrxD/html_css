Buffalo Replaced

Mitski

Track 02 • The Land Is Inhospitable and So Are We (2023, Dead Oceans)

Song Summary

"Buffalo Replaced" is a quiet, devastating meditation on the loss of nature to human progress. Mitski lists disappearing wonders — mosquitoes, fireflies, the full moon — all replaced by the relentless rumble of freight trains. The buffalo, once roaming vast plains in thunderous herds, are now gone, symbolized by the mechanical roar that fills the silence they left behind.

With gentle acoustic strumming and Mitski's soft, mournful delivery, the song evokes deep nostalgia for a wild America that's been paved over. It's not angry — it's resigned, aching, a lament for what we've erased in the name of expansion.

Key vibe: Gentle sorrow for a vanishing wilderness, the quiet cost of human dominance over the earth.
