Buffalo Replaced

Mitski

Track 02 • The Land Is Inhospitable and So Are We (2023, Dead Oceans)

Song Summary

A slow-burning portrait of numbness and emotional detachment. Mitski compares her heart to a buffalo replaced by machines — cold, mechanical, unable to feel. The sparse arrangement and her fragile delivery make the loneliness feel vast and irreversible.
