Bug Like an Angel

Mitski

Track 01 • The Land Is Inhospitable and So Are We (2023, Dead Oceans)

Song Summary

"Bug Like an Angel" opens the album with haunting vulnerability, using the image of a drowned bug in a drink as a metaphor for broken promises and the pull of addiction. The sudden entrance of a choir on the word "family" underscores the isolation and false comfort found in vices when real connection fails.

Mitski delivers the lyrics with raw honesty, blending personal pain with universal themes of faith, failure, and the search for something divine in the mundane. The sparse arrangement lets every word hit hard, setting the tone for an album full of quiet devastation and fleeting grace.

Key vibe: Divine in the broken, the struggle between self-destruction and the desperate need for salvation.
