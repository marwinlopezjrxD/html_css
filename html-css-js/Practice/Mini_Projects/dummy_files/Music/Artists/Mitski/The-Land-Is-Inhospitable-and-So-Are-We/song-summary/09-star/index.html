Star

Mitski

Track 09 • The Land Is Inhospitable and So Are We (2023, Dead Oceans)

Song Summary

"Star" is a beautiful, bittersweet reflection on love that lingers long after it's gone. Mitski compares the light of a star — reaching us years after it has died — to the feeling of a past lover still shining inside, even when the relationship has ended.

The song swells with orchestral warmth, capturing the quiet wonder of holding onto that distant glow. It's tender and cosmic, acknowledging that some connections burn brightly in memory, even across vast emotional distances.

Key vibe: Enduring love as a distant star — beautiful, unreachable, but still illuminating.
