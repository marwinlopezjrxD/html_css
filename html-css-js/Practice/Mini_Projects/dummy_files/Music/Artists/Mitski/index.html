About Mitski

Mitski Miyawaki (born September 27, 1990), known mononymously as Mitski, is a Japanese-American singer-songwriter celebrated for her introspective lyrics and genre-blending sound that spans indie rock, art pop, and chamber folk.

With albums like Be the Cowboy (2018) and The Land Is Inhospitable and So Are We (2023), she has earned critical acclaim for exploring themes of identity, longing, love, and isolation. Her emotionally raw performances and poetic songwriting have made her a defining voice in modern indie music.

Known for dramatic live shows and thoughtful storytelling, Mitski continues to captivate audiences worldwide.
