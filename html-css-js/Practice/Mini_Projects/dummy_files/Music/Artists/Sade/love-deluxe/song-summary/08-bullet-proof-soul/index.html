Bullet Proof Soul

Sade

Track 08 • Love Deluxe (1992, Epic)

Song Summary

"Bullet Proof Soul" is a warning about emotional armor — Sade sings of protecting the heart from pain, but questioning if it's worth the isolation. The funky groove contrasts the cautionary lyrics about love's risks.

It's about the struggle between vulnerability and self-preservation, wanting connection but fearing the wounds it brings.

Key vibe: Emotional self-protection, the cost of guarding the heart too tightly.
