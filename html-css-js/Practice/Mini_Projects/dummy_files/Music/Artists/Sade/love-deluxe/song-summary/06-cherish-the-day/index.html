Cherish the Day

Sade

Track 06 • Love Deluxe (1992, Epic)

Song Summary

"Cherish the Day" is a sensual, passionate embrace of the present moment in love — Sade urges savoring every touch and glance with a lover, living fully in the now before time slips away.

The sultry rhythm and intimate lyrics create a bedroom atmosphere, celebrating physical and emotional connection as something precious and fleeting.

Key vibe: Sensual presence in love, cherishing fleeting intimacy.
