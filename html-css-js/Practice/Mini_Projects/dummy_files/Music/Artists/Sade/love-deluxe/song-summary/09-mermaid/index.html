Mermaid

Sade

Track 09 • Love Deluxe (1992, Epic)

Song Summary

"Mermaid" is a dreamy instrumental closer (with subtle vocals), evoking ocean waves and mythical serenity. The atmospheric soundscape feels like drifting underwater, a peaceful escape from the album's emotional intensity.

It's a meditative breather, letting the listener float in quiet beauty before the album ends.

Key vibe: Dreamy serenity, peaceful escape, mythical calm.
