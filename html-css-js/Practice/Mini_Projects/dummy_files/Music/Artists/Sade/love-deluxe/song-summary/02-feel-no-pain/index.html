Feel No Pain

Sade

Track 02 • Love Deluxe (1992, Epic)

Song Summary

"Feel No Pain" is a compassionate plea for empathy in a harsh world — Sade addresses someone struggling with poverty and despair, urging them to hold on and promising better days. The smooth groove contrasts the heavy lyrics about economic hardship and survival.

It's a rare social commentary from Sade, highlighting quiet suffering and the need for human connection to ease the burden. The band encourages resilience without judgment, offering comfort in shared humanity.

Key vibe: Empathy for the struggling, hope amid hardship, quiet strength in adversity.
