Like a Tattoo

Sade

Track 04 • Love Deluxe (1992, Epic)

Song Summary

"Like a Tattoo" is a heartbreaking reflection on memories that never fade — painful experiences etched into the soul like permanent ink. Sade recalls lost love and regret with quiet sorrow, the past lingering no matter how time passes.

The sparse, haunting arrangement and Sade's vulnerable delivery amplify the sense of inescapable emotional scars. It's about carrying old wounds that shape who we are.

Key vibe: Permanent emotional scars, quiet regret, memories that mark the soul forever.
