Kiss of Life

Sade

Track 05 • Love Deluxe (1992, Epic)

Song Summary

"Kiss of Life" is an uplifting celebration of love's revitalizing power — Sade sings of a partner who breathes new energy into her world, making everything feel alive again. The breezy groove and joyful horns capture the rush of passion that awakens the soul.

It's a rare optimistic moment on the album, praising love as a life-giving force that chases away darkness and inspires growth.

Key vibe: Joyful renewal through love, passion as a life force.
