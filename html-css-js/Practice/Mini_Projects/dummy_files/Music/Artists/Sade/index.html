About Sade

Sade Adu (born Helen Folasade Adu, 16 January 1959), known professionally as Sade, is a British-Nigerian singer, songwriter, and the frontwoman of the Grammy-winning band Sade. With her smooth, soulful voice and sophisticated blend of jazz, soul, R&B, and pop, she has become one of the most iconic voices in music.

Debuting with Diamond Life in 1984, the band achieved global success with timeless hits like "Smooth Operator" and "Your Love Is King". Known for their elegant sound and long gaps between albums, Sade has released six studio albums over four decades, selling over 50 million records worldwide.

With effortless cool, emotional depth, and unmatched style, Sade remains a symbol of quiet luxury and enduring artistry.
