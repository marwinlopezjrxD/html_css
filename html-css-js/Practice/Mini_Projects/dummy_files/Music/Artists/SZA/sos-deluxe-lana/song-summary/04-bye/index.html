Bye

Lee Hi

Track 04 • 4 ONLY (2021, AOMG)

Song Summary

"Bye" is a catchy, empowering breakup anthem where Lee Hi decisively ends a selfish, one-sided relationship. With a groovy beat and confident delivery, she calls out her partner's egocentric demands ("Gimme love, gimme love—you're selfish") and chooses self-respect over lingering pain: "Bye, bye, bye, my love / I won't ever look back."

The lyrics paint a picture of moving on gracefully—leaving memories behind, wishing them well from afar, and reclaiming her peace ("I'm fine, don't worry about me"). Produced with an addictive rhythm, it showcases Lee Hi's versatile vocal range, dipping low and soaring effortlessly.

Key vibe: Liberating and cool-headed—turning heartbreak into a stylish exit, perfectly capturing the album's theme of navigating love's complexities.
