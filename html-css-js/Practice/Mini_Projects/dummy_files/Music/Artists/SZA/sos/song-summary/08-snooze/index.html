Song Summary

"Snooze" is a dreamy love song — SZA wants to hit snooze on life to stay in bed with her lover forever, savoring lazy mornings and intimacy. The smooth R&B production and tender vocals capture pure bliss in a relationship.

It's a rare sweet moment on the album, celebrating being fully present with someone special.

Key vibe: Lazy, loving mornings, wanting to pause time with a lover.
