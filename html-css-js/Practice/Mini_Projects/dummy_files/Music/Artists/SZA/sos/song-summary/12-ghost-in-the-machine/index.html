Ghost in the Machine

SZA

Track 12 • SOS (2022, Top Dawg / RCA)

Song Summary

"Ghost in the Machine" (feat. Phoebe Bridgers) is a haunting critique of modern disconnection — SZA feels like a ghost in digital relationships, craving real touch amid automated responses and superficial connections.

Bridgers' verse adds indie melancholy, amplifying the loneliness in a hyper-connected world.

Key vibe: Digital detachment, craving human touch in a machine world.
