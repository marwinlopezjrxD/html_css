Song Summary

"Forgiveless" is a gritty closer — SZA refuses to forgive betrayal, sampling Ol' Dirty Bastard for aggressive energy. The hard beat and unapologetic lyrics end the album on a hardened, no-mercy note.

It's about protecting peace by cutting off forgiveness for those who hurt deeply.

Key vibe: Refusal to forgive, hardened heart, aggressive closure.
