SZA

Track 03 • SOS (2022, Top Dawg / RCA)

Song Summary

"Seek & Destroy" is a fierce declaration of independence — SZA is done with toxic cycles and ready to burn it all down if needed. She's seeking peace but willing to destroy anything (or anyone) standing in her way, reclaiming her power with unapologetic energy.

The aggressive beat and defiant lyrics capture the moment of snapping — no more playing nice, just raw self-preservation.

Key vibe: Fierce liberation, destroying toxicity to find peace, unapologetic power move.
