Song Summary

"Good Days" is hopeful healing — SZA looks forward to better times, finding peace in small joys and letting go of past pain. The dreamy, uplifting production and positive lyrics mark a light at the end of the album's darkness.

It's about manifesting brighter days and embracing optimism after struggle.

Key vibe: Hopeful recovery, optimism after hardship, manifesting better days.
