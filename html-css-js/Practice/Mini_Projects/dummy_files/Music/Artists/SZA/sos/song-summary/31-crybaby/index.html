Song Summary

"Crybaby" is emotional vulnerability — SZA owns her sensitivity, crying over love and life without shame. The tender production and raw lyrics embrace tears as part of her strength.

It's about rejecting the idea that crying is weak — tears are human, and she's proud of feeling deeply.
