Too Late

SZA

Track 17 • SOS (2022, Top Dawg / RCA)

Song Summary

"Too Late" is regret over realizing a relationship's end too late — SZA reflects on missed chances to save it, accepting the finality with sorrow. The slow groove mirrors the lingering pain.

It's about timing in love, when effort comes after the damage is done.

Key vibe: Regret over bad timing, acceptance of irreversible loss.
