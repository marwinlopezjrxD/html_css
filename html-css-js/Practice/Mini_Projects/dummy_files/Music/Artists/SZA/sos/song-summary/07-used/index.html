Song Summary

"Used" (feat. Don Toliver) is a sultry, bitter reflection on casual hookups — SZA feels discarded after being intimate, questioning if she was just used for the moment. Toliver's verse adds the male perspective, both admitting the emptiness.

It's raw about post-hookup regret and feeling disposable in modern dating.

Key vibe: Post-intimacy emptiness, feeling used and discarded.
