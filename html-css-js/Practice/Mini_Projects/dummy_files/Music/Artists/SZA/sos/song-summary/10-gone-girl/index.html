Gone Girl

SZA

Track 10 • SOS (2022, Top Dawg / RCA)

Song Summary

"Gone Girl" is SZA embracing independence — becoming the "gone girl" who disappears after heartbreak, reclaiming her power by vanishing. The dreamy production contrasts the fierce self-preservation.

It's empowerment through absence — no more chasing, just peace in solitude.

Key vibe: Empowerment through vanishing, self-preservation after heartbreak.
