Song Summary

"Diamond Boy (DTM)" is SZA celebrating a rare, valuable man — calling him her "diamond in the rough" who stands out in a sea of average. The confident, bouncy beat and playful lyrics flip the script on usual heartbreak narratives.

It's about appreciating a partner who treats her right and shines bright — "DTM" = "Do The Most".

Key vibe: Celebrating a rare good man, appreciation in love.
