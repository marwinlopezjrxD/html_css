I Hate U

SZA

Track 21 • SOS (2022, Top Dawg / RCA)

Song Summary

"I Hate U" is conflicted post-breakup emotion — SZA vents hating an ex for moving on, but admits missing them and the complicated feelings that linger. The moody production and raw delivery capture the love-hate cycle.

It's honest about the messiness of healing, where resentment and longing coexist.

Key vibe: Love-hate post-breakup, emotional contradiction.
