Blind

SZA

Track 06 • SOS (2022, Top Dawg / RCA)

Song Summary

"Blind" is SZA admitting she turned a blind eye to red flags — ignoring truth for comfort in a toxic relationship. The stripped-back production and vulnerable vocals lay bare the self-deception that kept her stuck.

It's honest about choosing illusion over reality to avoid pain, even when deep down she knew better.

Key vibe: Willful ignorance in love, self-deception for comfort.
