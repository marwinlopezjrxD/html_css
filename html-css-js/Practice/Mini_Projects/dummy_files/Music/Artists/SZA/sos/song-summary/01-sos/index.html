Song Summary

"SOS" opens the album with a desperate cry for help — SZA pleads for rescue from her own emotional chaos, feeling lost at sea in relationships and self-doubt. The distorted voicemail samples and haunting production set the tone for an album full of vulnerability and urgency.

It's a raw introduction to SZA's inner turmoil, blending frustration with a need for connection in a world that feels overwhelming.

Key vibe: Cry for help, emotional distress signal, searching for salvation amid chaos.
