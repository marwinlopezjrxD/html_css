Waterride

Lee Hi

Track 03 • 4 ONLY (2021, AOMG)

Song Summary

"Waterride" (물타기) is an upbeat, experimental track with fast-tempo rhythmic guitar and playful energy. The title refers to "watering down" or dodging issues—Lee Hi calls out a partner who nitpicks, twists words, and avoids direct communication whenever she tries to speak up.

With biting, sarcastic lyrics like "Are these words hard to get through or are you dumb?" and "You bite and hang on whenever I say something," it's a fun yet sharp take on frustrating arguments and evasive behavior in relationships. The infectious "clap your hands" hook and groovy vibe make it one of the album's most addictive, lighthearted moments.

Key vibe: Sassy and rhythmic—Lee Hi's velvety vocals dance over the beat, turning annoyance into pure endorphin rush.
