About SZA

Solána Imani Rowe (born November 8, 1989), known professionally as SZA, is an American singer-songwriter celebrated for her raw, confessional lyrics and genre-blending sound that fuses R&B, neo-soul, alternative, and pop.

Debuting with the critically acclaimed Ctrl (2017), she explored love, insecurity, and self-worth with unmatched vulnerability. Her sophomore album SOS (2022) became a cultural phenomenon, blending introspective storytelling with infectious hooks and earning multiple Grammys.

With her ethereal voice, poetic honesty, and fearless exploration of womanhood, SZA has redefined modern R&B and become one of the most influential artists of her generation.
